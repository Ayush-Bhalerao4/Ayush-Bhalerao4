## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/iY8CRBdQXODJSCERIr/giphy.gif" width="30"> About

I'm a **data-driven analyst** who transforms messy datasets into clear, actionable insights that help businesses make better decisions. My work sits at the intersection of **data analytics, business intelligence, and financial analysis** — from building interactive dashboards that stakeholders actually use, to running statistical models that uncover hidden trends.

My engineering background in AI & Data Science gives me an edge: I don't just analyze data — I build the **pipelines, dashboards, and ML models** around it. Recent projects include a **sales intelligence dashboard** processing 8,500+ grocery records with multi-dimensional analysis views, **computer-vision-driven disaster risk scoring**, and **graph neural networks** for molecular property prediction.

I combine strong **Python & SQL foundations** with visualization tools like **Power BI, Tableau, and Plotly** to deliver end-to-end analytics solutions — from data wrangling and EDA to polished reports and presentations.

**Open to:** `Data Analyst` · `Business Analyst` · `Financial Analyst` — happy to relocate.
